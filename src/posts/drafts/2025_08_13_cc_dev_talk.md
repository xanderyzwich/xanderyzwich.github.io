Paths in Tech: Real Roles, Real Stories.  
It's an event designed to shared a broad spectrum of what tech careers can look like
--- 

As a developer there are several factors that will have a very large impact on your day to day life. 

One is your field of focus, whether it be frontend, backend, CICD, database, or something platform specific like SalesForce, Shopify, WordPress, or similar. 

Another major factor will be the size of your company/team. I personally work for a company of around fifty people and a team of four developers counting myself. This substantially small upstart type headcount allows me to have a much faster and more freestyle approach to a LOT of things. I've worked in larger companies like FedEx before and things were much different, the teams were more isolated and focused. 

Another major factor will be the intake and delivery processes that you have for work. In the most ideal of scenarios you would have a seasoned product owner that understands how developers work, breaks down tasks in small pieces, and has a clear definition of done. Other times you'll wind up fighting to overcome one or more of those things by trying to get things broken down into smaller deliverable chunks, fix broken expectations, adjust the work to the expectations, or fight to figure out what actually determines that you've been successful. The problem with this is that you'll most likely not understand why or how to do many of these until you've seen how wrong things can go from not doing them. 

After you've gone through getting your tasks, getting them defined well, and attending any mandatory meetings, you'll get to work on some code. At some point during that process you're gonna smack headlong into a problem that you don't understand and much of the time you might not even know what to search for to find the answer.
