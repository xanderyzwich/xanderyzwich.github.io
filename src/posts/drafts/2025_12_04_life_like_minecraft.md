When you start in minecraft's survival mode, you have nothing. You have to punch trees to get wood before you can craft logs into planks and then a crafting table. Only then can you craft even the most simple of tools. You don't get the best tools right away. You have to use the most basic tools so that you can slowly acquire materials needed to make better ones. This is like life. When we start we have nothing, but we have parents that will help us to acquire the basics. They'll help us understand what steps to take to begin progressing. They can't fight every creeper than you come across for you, because they are busy playing their own game. But, they can help you to craft some armor, make sure that you have tools, and send you into the world with something. It is up to you to use those tools, and learn to wield them well. You must learn what's important to you, and figure out how to move towards your goals.
